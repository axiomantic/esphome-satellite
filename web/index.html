esphome-satellite

Rock-solid, glitch-free on-device state supervisor for ESPHome and Home Assistant voice satellites.

  1. Connect your voice satellite board (e.g. Seeed ReSpeaker XVF3800) via USB.
  2. Click Install esphome-satellite below using Chrome, Edge, or an install-capable browser.
  3. Select the serial port, follow the prompts, and configure your Wi-Fi.
  4. Home Assistant will automatically discover your satellite!
Your browser does not support WebSerial. Please use Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge on desktop. WebSerial permission denied. Please allow serial access to flash.
