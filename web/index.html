esphome-satellite Web Installer

On-device state supervisor for ESPHome and Home Assistant voice satellites with hardware target switching, multi-wake-word selection, and customizable audio feedback.

Seeed ReSpeaker XVF3800 with 4-mic array and hardware acoustic echo cancellation
ESP32-S3 hardware neural accelerator runs up to 3 wake word models concurrently in parallel. Add slots to configure multiple active wake words.

WHAT TO DO NEXT

Next Steps: Connecting to Home Assistant

1

Reboot Board & Connect to Wi-Fi

⚠️ Essential for ESP32-S3 Native USB: Because this board uses native USB, software resets cannot exit the ROM bootloader after flashing or erasing. Unplug and reconnect the USB-C cable (or tap the RST button) once before configuring Wi-Fi! If you click before rebooting, it will report "Improv Wi-Fi Serial not detected" because ESPHome has not booted yet.

Once replugged, connect your device to Wi-Fi using either method below:

Method B (Fallback Hotspot): Connect your phone or laptop to the open Wi-Fi network Satellite Fallback Hotspot. The captive portal at http://192.168.4.1 opens automatically to enter your Wi-Fi credentials.
2

Add in Home Assistant

Open Home Assistant and navigate to Settings → Devices & Services. Your new esphome-satellite Web Installer will appear highlighted at the top under Discovered.

Click Configure, then click Submit (no encryption key required).

Add to Home Assistant
3

Customize Device & Audio Presets

On the device card, you can customize your feedback styles, wake chimes, and runtime settings anytime directly from Home Assistant.

↻

Testing Clean Flow / Factory Reset

If your device previously had another Wi-Fi network or an old encryption key saved in NVS memory, click Erase Device (Factory Reset) above or run esptool.py erase_flash in terminal before clicking Install Firmware.
