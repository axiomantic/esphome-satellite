WHAT TO DO NEXT

Next Steps: Connecting to Home Assistant

1

Reboot Board & Connect to Wi-Fi

Important for ESP32-S3 Native USB: Because this board uses native USB, software resets cannot exit the ROM bootloader after flashing or erasing. Unplug and reconnect the USB-C cable (or tap the RST button) once before configuring Wi-Fi! If you click before rebooting, it will report "Improv Wi-Fi Serial not detected" because ESPHome has not booted yet.

Once replugged, connect your device to Wi-Fi using either method below:

Method B (Fallback Hotspot): Connect your phone or laptop to the open Wi-Fi network Satellite Fallback Hotspot. The captive portal at http://192.168.4.1 opens automatically to enter your Wi-Fi credentials.
2

Add in Home Assistant

Open Home Assistant and navigate to Settings → Devices & Services. Your new device will appear under Discovered as Voice Satellite (e.g. Voice Satellite ba2c6c).

Click Configure, then click Submit.

No Encryption Key Needed: This firmware connects without an API encryption key.
If Home Assistant prompts for an Encryption Key:
Brand-new boards ship with Seeed's encrypted factory firmware. If Home Assistant discovered the board before it was flashed, it cached a requirement for an encryption key and will reject a blank field with "not all required fields are filled in".
Quick Fix: Cancel the prompt, click Add Integration → ESPHome, and enter your device IP (or esphome-satellite-<mac>.local) and port 6053. It connects directly with zero encryption prompts!

Customize Device & Audio Presets
