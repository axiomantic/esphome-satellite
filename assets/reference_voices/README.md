# Reference Voice Presets (F5-TTS Zero-Shot Cloning)

This directory provides clean, natural human voice clips and exact reference transcripts used by F5-TTS to clone target wake words across diverse speaker timbres.

All audio clips are normalized to 16,000 Hz mono 16-bit PCM WAV and peak-normalized to -1.0 dBFS true peak.

## Standard Shared Transcript (Tortoise and Hare Narration)
Once upon a time, there was a Hare who never stopped bragging about how incredibly fast he was. "I am the fastest animal in the entire forest! No one can beat me. I guarantee it, 100 percent!" The Tortoise, who was completely exhausted from hearing this day after day, finally challenged him to a race. The Hare just laughed. "You? A race against me? I will be at the finish line before you even take your very first step!" The next morning, the race began. The Hare dashed off like a lightning bolt, leaving a massive, swirling cloud of dust behind him. After a few minutes, he looked back. The Tortoise was nowhere in sight, so the Hare decided to take a quick, comfortable nap under a large oak tree. Meanwhile, the Tortoise just kept walking. Step by step, inch by inch. He never stopped, and he never gave up. Hours passed. The sun started to go down. Suddenly, the Hare woke up with a loud gasp! He scrambled to his feet and sprinted just as fast as his legs could possibly carry him toward the finish line. But as he finally got there, he froze. The Tortoise was already crossing the ribbon, smiling a very slow, very happy smile. Slow and steady really did win the race.
